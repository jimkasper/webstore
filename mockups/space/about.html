About me

I'm a product designer based in Hamburg. I've spent the last seven years figuring out how to make software that people actually want to use — not just software that works.

Outside of work I'm an obsessive reader (books only — I can't do articles), a mediocre but enthusiastic runner, and someone who thinks too much about decisions. I keep a journal. I'm slowly writing a book about design and uncertainty. I have opinions about coffee.

I built this SPACE because I wanted somewhere that felt like mine. Somewhere the algorithm doesn't decide what matters. If you're here, you chose to be.
