Overview

This note tracks my thinking process for the Berlin project decision. Brandt & Co. have offered a 6-month secondment to their Berlin office, leading a new product team. Decision needed by May 1, 2026.

Related: [[Team research]] · [[Cost of living comparison]] · [[Contacts in Berlin]]

Framework

Using a modified version of the [[Regret Minimization Framework]] (Jeff Bezos) combined with a pros/cons weighted matrix.

Key questions

  • Will I regret not going in 10 years?
  • What specifically am I afraid of — and are those fears rational?
  • What would I need to be true for this to be a clear yes?

Weighted factors

  1. Career growth (weight: 30%) — High. Leading a team at this level is rare at this stage.
  2. Financial (weight: 20%) — Neutral. Salary bump covers Berlin's higher cost of living. See [[Cost of living comparison]].
  3. Relationships (weight: 25%) — Risk. 6 months away from existing relationships. Jamie is supportive but concerned.
  4. Reversibility (weight: 15%) — High. 6-month contract. Clear exit. This is not permanent.
  5. Personal growth (weight: 10%) — High. New city, new culture, new challenge.
"The hesitation is just routine anxiety, I think. I've felt this before every good decision I've made."
— Morning journal, Apr 24

Current position

Leaning yes, but want to have an honest conversation with Jamie before deciding. Action: draft a clear picture of what a yes looks like logistically. Write [[decision journal entry]] by Apr 25.
