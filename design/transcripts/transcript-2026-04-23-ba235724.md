---

**Followers vs. Connections — separate systems:**

*Following (one-way):*
- Follow a profile to access their public and follower-visible content
- No mutual obligation — the followed user doesn't need to follow back
- Followers see what the poster explicitly allows followers to see
- No access to agreement app, relationship health score, or deeper profile sections
- Can be blocked or removed silently

*Connections (mutual):*
- Both parties must agree — request and acceptance
- Deeper profile access as set by each user independently
- Enables the agreement app between connected users
- Feeds the relationship health score
- Tagged in journal entries, tasks, and events
- Visible in data-driven blocks — "Best Friends," "Most Time With"
- Can be categorized — close connections, professional, family, etc.
- Each category can have independent visibility and access rules

---

**Reposting — privacy chain:**
- Original poster sets shareability on every piece of content — not shareable, connections only, followers, public
- If sharing is allowed, reposters inherit the original's permission ceiling — they can only restrict further, never expand
- Example: original allows "connections only" sharing → reposter can restrict to "close connections" but cannot open it to "public"
- Each repost in the chain logs who shared it and when — full audit trail visible to the original poster
- Original poster can revoke shareability at any time — all downstream reposts immediately respect the new setting
- Revoking does not delete reposts already made but removes visibility of the content within them

---

**Ephemeral content — both formats:**

*Stories:*
- 24-hour disappearing content — standard format
- Visible to followers, connections, or custom audience per story
- Viewers visible to the poster (with privacy tier rules applied)
- Reacting and replying available within the 24-hour window
- Can be saved to own vault before expiry — never recoverable after

*Custom expiry:*
- Any post or media can have a user-set lifespan — 1 hour, 7 days, 30 days, custom date
- After expiry: permanently and cryptographically deleted — consistent with zero-knowledge architecture
- User notified before expiry with option to extend or save to vault
- Agreements and journal cross-references to expired content flagged gracefully

---

**Feed algorithm — fully user-controlled and tradeable:**

*User control:*
- Chronological available as a baseline — always an option
- Full customization of weighting parameters:
  - Content type preference (video, text, audio, long-form)
  - Connection type weighting (close connections weighted higher than general followers)
  - Recency vs. engagement balance
  - Topic and `#tag` preferences
  - Persona filtering — see content from specific personas only
  - Mute, filter, and block rules built into the algorithm
- Multiple feed algorithms saveable and switchable instantly — different algorithms for different moods or contexts
- Full transparency — user can see exactly what their algorithm is doing at any time

*Marketplace:*
- Feed algorithms fully tradeable in the marketplace — same as blocks, modules, and themes
- Algorithms listed with transparent descriptions of exactly what they do — no hidden manipulation
- zk-STARKs proof that the algorithm performs as advertised — buyers can verify before purchasing
- Ratings and reviews from other users
- Creator earns credits or privacy coin per sale
- Featured and trending feed algorithms surfaced in the store
- Users can fork a purchased algorithm — customize it and republish as their own variation with attribution to the original

*Why this matters:*
- Direct counterpoint to opaque platform-controlled algorithmic feeds
- No hidden engagement manipulation — the algorithm is yours, transparent, and auditable
- The feed serves the user, not the platform's engagement metrics
- A genuinely unique and marketable feature
