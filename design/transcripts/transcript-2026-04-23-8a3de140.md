**Assistant:** **On burning** — since the supply is revenue-matched, it already self-regulates (minted when revenue grows, theoretically retired when it shrinks). Burning adds a second deflationary lever on top of that:

- **No burn** — supply stays clean, value tied purely to revenue backing. Simplest.
- **Fee burn** — a small % of every transaction fee (e.g. 0.1–0.5%) is permanently destroyed. Creates mild deflationary pressure over time; rewards holders as circulating supply slowly shrinks. The platform still has the full revenue backing, so burned coins just mean remaining coins are worth slightly more.
- **Governance burn** — if the council decides supply is bloated (e.g. revenue dipped after a high-growth period), they vote to destroy coins from a reserve. More surgical, but slower and political.

Fee burn pairs well with the tiered fee structure you already chose — higher tiers pay less in fees, so they contribute less to the burn but benefit from it equally. Governance burn pairs well with your existing governance model.
