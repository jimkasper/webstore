**Assistant:** Good shift — this is foundational since every other system (valuation engine, algorithm marketplace, tier rules) depends on knowing exactly what data exists and how it enters the system.

To frame the conversation, there are a few distinct categories worth thinking through separately:

- **Behavioral** — how users interact with the apps (clicks, patterns, frequency, timing)
- **Social** — connections, interaction patterns, relationship strength signals
- **Content** — what they write, post, or create
- **Metadata** — timestamps, device info, session lengths (often more revealing than content)
- **Transactional** — actions taken (passwords saved, capsules created, algorithms run)
- **Inferred** — patterns we derive from raw data (habits, decision tendencies, life stage signals)
- **Third-party** — data imported from outside the suite (financial accounts, health apps, calendars, etc.)

A few questions to guide where you want to start:

1. Does the data collection extend beyond our own apps — do we want users to be able to pull in outside data sources (bank feeds, fitness trackers, etc.) into their data hub?
2. Is inferred/derived data treated the same as raw data for tier and valuation purposes?
3. Are there categories you want to explicitly **never** collect regardless of tier?
