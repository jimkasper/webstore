**Assistant:** Clean, well-reasoned decisions. Capturing them formally:

**Upgrade — previous data:**
- We retain it, but present the user with two options at time of upgrade:
  - **Buy us out**: Pay a fee to have their previously collected data permanently deleted
  - **Anonymize it**: Free option — data is stripped of identifying information and retained in aggregate only
- Until they choose, data sits under its original collection terms

**Downgrade — connections and historical data:**
- Higher-tier connections are notified with full tier detail — they then decide to keep, suspend, or remove the connection
- **All previously created data is permanently governed by the tier it was created under** — downgrading never retroactively loosens protections on past data
  - Content created at Tier 1 stays zero-knowledge forever, even if the user is now Tier 5
  - Only new data created after the downgrade follows the new tier's rules
- This means every piece of data in the system needs to be tagged with the tier it was created under — that tag is immutable

**No grace period:**
- New tier rules apply to new data immediately upon switching
- Billing and features also change immediately

This "data created under Tier X stays governed by Tier X forever" principle is actually a strong trust and marketing point — users can downgrade without fearing their past is exposed. Worth making it a visible promise in your terms and UI.
