**User:** Can we sell domains?

**Assistant:** Yes, and it fits naturally into the suite. Here's how it works and what's involved:

**Two paths to selling domains:**

**Registrar (full):**
- Directly accredited by ICANN — register domains at the source
- Full control, best margins
- Significant cost, infrastructure, and compliance overhead to get accredited
- Long-term goal once the platform is established

**Reseller (practical starting point):**
- Partner with an existing accredited registrar (Namecheap, Enom, ResellerClub)
- Sell domains under your brand, they handle the backend
- Much lower barrier to entry, faster to launch
- Revenue share with the registrar partner
- Domains purchasable with platform credits or privacy coin

---

**Why it fits the suite perfectly:**

- Users setting up the **cloud installer** need a domain — sell it to them at the same time
- **Profile pages** can have custom domains attached
- **Self-hosted installations** need domains and DNS management
- **Browser** users naturally think about domains
- Becomes a one-stop setup experience — buy domain, install nTogether, configure profile, all in one flow

**The privacy angle as a differentiator:**
- WHOIS privacy protection enabled by default on every domain purchased — most registrars charge extra for this
- Fully consistent with the platform's privacy-first philosophy
- A genuine selling point against GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.

**Additional domain services to consider:**
- DNS management built into the suite dashboard
- SSL certificates auto-provisioned (Let's Encrypt integration)
- Domain expiry reminders surfaced in the scheduler and journal
- Domain costs tracked automatically in the financial function

**The reseller path is the right starting point** — launch fast, build volume, pursue full ICANN accreditation once the platform has scale.

**User:** We should also offer hosting

**Assistant:** Natural extension — domains and hosting together create a complete web presence package. Here's the full picture:

**Hosting types to offer:**

| Type | Description | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Shared | Multiple sites on one server | Entry level, low traffic |
| VPS | Dedicated resources, shared hardware | Growing sites, developers |
| Dedicated | Full physical server | High traffic, maximum control |
| Cloud | Scalable, pay-as-you-go | Variable traffic, enterprises |
| nTogether-managed | Pre-configured for the suite | Users running their own nTogether instance |

**Two paths — same as domains:**

**Infrastructure owner (long-term):**
- Own data centers or lease rack space
- Full control, best margins, maximum privacy guarantees
- Significant upfront capital and operational overhead

**Reseller (starting point):**
- White-label infrastructure from providers like Hetzner, DigitalOcean, or Vultr
- Branded as nTogether hosting, they handle the hardware
- Fast to launch, proven infrastructure, lower risk
