**Morning Entry:**
- Sleep duration (hours/minutes)
- Sleep quality (rating scale)
- Intended wake time vs. actual wake time
- Energy level (emoji selector)
- Dream journal (free-form text with full tagging — `@`, `@@`, `#`, `:`)
- Three distinct lists:
  - **Need-do** — must happen today
  - **Should-do** — important but flexible
  - **To-do** — general intentions

---

**Nightly Entry:**
- Overall feelings about the day (free-form text with tagging)
- **Activities log** (multiple entries, each capturing):
  - What they did
  - Where (location tag)
  - Who with (`@contact` / `@@group`)
  - Money spent
  - Overall feeling about the event
- **Food log** (multiple entries, each capturing):
  - What they ate
  - Cost
  - How it was (rating + optional notes)
- **Missed event prompt catch-up**:
  - Any scheduled event prompts left unanswered surface here
  - User can fill them in retroactively or push to another day
- **Tomorrow's list**:
  - Tasks with necessity level (need/should/want)
  - Feeds directly into the next morning's entry

- Sleep duration + quality + wake time delta → energy level correlation
- Energy level → productivity and decision quality throughout the day
- Food cost + rating → spending pattern and wellbeing correlation
- Money spent on activities + feeling about them → value-per-dollar life satisfaction metric
- Missed prompts pushed forward → procrastination and avoidance pattern tracking
- Tomorrow's necessity levels vs. what actually gets done → follow-through analysis

All of this feeds directly into decision evaluation, outcome tracking, and future projection.

**User:** The todo, should do and need-do lists should probably be its own separate app function, that coordinates with those journal entry’s.

When adding a do the user should be prompted to provide estimates of time, cost, location, and people implicated with it

**Assistant:** Captured. The task management system becomes its own dedicated app function with bidirectional coordination with the journal.

**Task creation prompt — every new entry across all three lists asks for:**
- **Time estimate** — how long they expect it to take
- **Cost estimate** — expected spend
- **Location** — where it will happen
- **People implicated** — `@contact` / `@@group` tags

**Coordination with the journal:**
- Morning entry pulls that day's need-do, should-do, and to-do lists directly from the task app — no re-entry
- Nightly entry surfaces incomplete tasks for review, rescheduling, or pushback
- Completed tasks auto-populate the nightly activity log as a starting point

**Coordination with the scheduler:**
- Time estimates + location feed directly into the calendar for automatic scheduling suggestions
- People tagged on tasks can receive associated event invites through the platform

**Why the estimates matter for analytics:**
- **Time estimate vs. actual** — tracks planning accuracy and identifies consistently underestimated task types
- **Cost estimate vs. actual** — financial planning accuracy over time
- **Location patterns** — where the user spends their time and energy
- **People implicated** — which contacts appear most in their obligations and what that costs them in time and money
- **Estimate accuracy by necessity level** — do need-dos get estimated more accurately than to-dos?

This becomes one of the richest data sources in the entire suite for decision evaluation and future projection.
